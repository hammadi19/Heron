# Heron Marketing Design Guide

## Direction

Heron Marketing uses a light editorial agency style: oversized typography, generous whitespace, sharp content grids, soft pastel atmosphere, and black conversion-focused calls to action.

## Logo

- Primary mark: abstract heron line symbol with electric-blue accent.
- Wordmark: uppercase `HERON` with wide-tracked `MARKETING` subtitle.
- Use the full logo in navigation and footer.
- Use the compact mark in badges, stamps, and small CTA lockups.

## Palette

- Ink: `#080808`
- Paper: `#FBFAF8`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Electric blue: `#145CFF`
- Aqua: `#7CE8D9`
- Coral: `#FF7058`
- Lavender accent: `#9D75FF`
- Soft text: `#666666`
- Fine borders: `rgba(0,0,0,.08)` to `rgba(0,0,0,.12)`

## Typography

- Main UI font: Geist Sans.
- Editorial accent: serif italic, used only for large hero/quote moments.
- Headlines are oversized, low line-height, and normal letter spacing.
- Body copy stays compact, readable, and neutral.

## Layout

- Desktop max width: `92rem`.
- Sections use clean horizontal spacing and thin separators.
- Avoid heavy nested cards and dashboard-style blocks.
- Use rectangular modules with subtle radius only for media, CTAs, FAQ panels, and final CTA.

## Components

- Header: white, bordered, centered navigation, search icon, black `Get In Touch` CTA, circular burger.
- Hero: mixed serif/sans headline, pastel glow, right-side rounded lifestyle image, circular trust badge.
- Services: icon columns separated by thin vertical rules.
- Process: image left, four-step editorial timeline right.
- Work: three image-led tiles with dark overlay and circular arrow action.
- Proof: white metric band with large serif numbers.
- FAQ: minimal accordion rows with plus icon.
- CTA: black panel with aqua/blue glow and white button.

## Responsive

- Hero stacks on mobile.
- CTAs become full-width where useful.
- Large type scales by breakpoint, never with viewport units.
- No horizontal scrolling at mobile widths.
